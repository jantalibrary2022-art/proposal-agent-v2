NBCFDC — Format of Project Proposal for Grant under CSR

The applicant must submit the project proposal in the following prescribed format.

PART I: PROJECT PROPOSAL SUBMISSION FORMAT (13 sections, in this order)
1. Title of the project
2. Current Socio-economic status (assessment of beneficiary selection across trades; skill levels Skilled/Semi-Skilled/Unskilled; income; gender composition)
3. Objectives of Project
4. Executive summary of proposal
5. Targeted group of the project proposal
6. Geographical area of execution (including rationale for area selection)
7. Project Cost, in three subsections:
   (A) Budget details, with an "Activities Budget Time Line" table showing start date, end date, number of days, and totals
   (B) Means of Finance breakdown
   (C) Funding requirements at project start, middle and end phases
8. Execution/Implementation schedule (with specific time-frame)
9. Human Resources (district-level committee composition with names, designations, contact details and email addresses)
10. Expected outcome/benefits (outcomes regarding skill and income improvements upon completion)
11. Monitoring plan (timeline for monitoring during and after execution)
12. Sustainability committee composition (target-group representatives to assume project responsibility post-handover)
13. Project Coordinator details (name, designation, office address, phone, email of authorised representative)

PART II: REQUIRED ANNEXURES
Undertaking, registration certificates, audited accounts (three years), PAN/TAN numbers, board member list, shareholder information, and a Monthly Progress Report template.
