SBI Foundation (SBIF)
Request for Proposals: Climate Smart Livelihoods

About: SBI Foundation invites proposals from eligible non-profit organisations for CSR support under its focus on Climate Smart Livelihoods. SBIF supports work across the following focus areas: Livelihoods & Entrepreneurship, Education, Environment, Sports, Rural Development, Healthcare, Disability & Inclusion, and Women Empowerment.

Target groups: Small and marginal farmers in climate-vulnerable areas, tribal communities, landless farmers, women, youth, persons with disabilities, and members of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) and Common Interest Groups (CIGs).

Geography: India. The applicant organisation must be registered in India and must have an existing presence in the state where the project is proposed.

Financial support: Rs. 50.00 Lakhs to Rs. 5.00 Crore, for a support period of 1 to 3 years, starting in the current financial year.

Eligibility:
- Registered non-profit with a minimum of 3 years of organisational existence.
- Minimum 3 years of experience in the proposed thematic area, with presence in the proposed state.
- Cumulative annual revenue of Rs. 2 Crore or more for proposals of Rs. 50-100 Lakh; Rs. 5 Crore or more for larger proposals.
- Minimum 3 board members and 5 permanent employees.
- Completed at least 3 similar projects funded by public, government or foundation sources.
- No political or religious affiliations.

Ineligibility: An individual donor cannot be among the organisation's top 3 donors. Failure to furnish due diligence documents will lead to rejection.

Evaluation: Preference to organisations demonstrating measurable outcomes to ensure effectiveness and efficiency, and a proven track record of designing and implementing climate smart livelihoods projects.

Submission: Proposals along with the due diligence documents listed in Annexure II. No prescribed proposal format is specified in this call.

Deadline: 14 June 2025.
